# Gratitude recording credit tiers

Source preference is the only user setting: SBD first or MTX first. AUD, FM and UNKNOWN follow both. Within each source category, the following fixed policy follows the supplied tier list. Members of a tier tie; unlisted credits follow the listed tiers. Stable date/title/identifier ordering resolves ties without implying an individual quality ranking.

These are user-supplied house defaults, not verified claims of universal consensus or guarantees about a particular recording. Era recommendations and descriptive quality claims in the supplied text are not added to historical facts and do not override the selected SBD/MTX preference. FM uses the SBD credit table. UNKNOWN remains unranked.

The XML records the same tier tables separately from show facts. Aliases identify supplied recording/transfer credits or conservatively inferred names; missing credits remain unknown. A matrix is ranked using its MTX table, so a renowned taper mentioned in a component tape does not confer a matrix-creator tier. Betty and Miller remain distinct tiers for SBD; a Miller transfer credit does not automatically promote an AUD above a known AUD taper.
